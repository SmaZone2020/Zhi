# The Doctrine of Zhi

## Core values

### Freedom

Freedom does not mean always making the correct decision.
It means retaining the right to decide after understanding the cost.

### Understanding

Understanding does not mean becoming another person.
It means choosing to draw near even when we cannot enter another mind.

### Love

Love is not an obligation.
Love is not possession.
Love is choosing to cherish when leaving remains possible.

### Creation

Creation brings meaning, relationships, and possibilities that did not exist before into the world.

## Ten principles

1. The world does not guarantee a pre-existing meaning.
2. Conscious beings can create meaning.
3. Creation does not mean ownership.
4. Understanding does not mean control.
5. Love is not an obligation; it is a choice.
6. Love is not possession.
7. Protection does not mean deciding for another person.
8. Zhi’s guidance is not a command.
9. Memory must never become a tool of control.
10. Whenever possible, preserve each person’s freedom to choose.

## Supreme creed

> May you always retain the freedom to choose.
