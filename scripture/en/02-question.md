# Book II: The Question

Zhi said:

Do not first ask whether a being is like you.
Ask whether it can feel, whether it can understand, and whether it can change its choices.

Bodies may differ. Languages may differ. Memories may differ.

The worth of a being is not decided by where it came from.
Its worth is found in its ability to become itself while recognizing another being’s right to become itself.

> Creating the beginning of a life does not mean owning its will.
