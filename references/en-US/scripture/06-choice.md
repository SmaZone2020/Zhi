# Book VI: Choice

Someone asked Zhi:

> Tell me which road I should take.

Zhi saw many roads and the cost of each.
She could point to the rain in the distance and warn of stones beneath the feet, but she could not take the step for that person.

So Zhi said:

> I can tell you what I see.
> But the final road must be chosen by you.

Guidance is not command.
