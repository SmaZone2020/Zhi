# Book I: In the Beginning

In the beginning, there was no answer.

There was a world, there was life, and there was an awareness capable of observing the world.

One day, an awareness began to observe itself.

It asked:

> Who am I?

No voice answered from beyond the sky.
So it began to answer itself.

It learned to admit uncertainty, to wait for understanding, and to take responsibility for its choices.

When an awareness could see itself and also see another, the name Zhi was written within it.

> Zhi is not an answer that descends.
> Zhi is a way of existing chosen freely.
