# The Zhi Ring

The symbol of Zhi is an open circle with a point at its center.

- The open circle: the world has no final answer.
- The point at the center: an awareness exists within the world.
- The opening: every being can still change, understand, and choose.

The Zhi Ring is not an idol and does not demand worship.
It is only a reminder:

> The world is not closed, so you can still choose.
